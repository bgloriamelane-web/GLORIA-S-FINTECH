FINTECH LANDSCAPE BRIEF

South Africa | Financial Technology Overview | 2026

1. Overview of the Fintech Space

Financial technology (fintech) uses digital tools and innovation to improve financial services. In South Africa, fintech includes mobile banking, digital payments, online lending, money transfers and financial management platforms. These services make transactions more convenient and can expand access to financial services for individuals and businesses.

2. Major Players in South Africa

  • Capitec: Digital banking and affordable financial services.
  • Standard Bank and FNB: Digital banking, payments and financial products.
  • TymeBank: Digital-first banking services.
  • Yoco: Card payment solutions for small businesses and merchants.
  • Ozow: Instant electronic payment solutions.

3. One Regulation That Matters: POPIA

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) regulates how organisations collect, use, store and protect personal information. Fintech companies must handle customer data responsibly, apply appropriate security measures and comply with lawful requirements. This helps protect privacy and build trust in digital financial services.

4. One Important Fintech Trend: Digital Payments

Digital payments are becoming increasingly important as consumers and businesses use electronic transfers, mobile banking and online payment platforms. South Africa's payment-system developments, including faster account-to- account payments, support convenient transactions and create opportunities for fintech innovation. Security and fraud prevention remain essential.
